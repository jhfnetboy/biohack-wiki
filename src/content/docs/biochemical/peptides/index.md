## Six directions

These headings are how the wiki files pages. They are not a claim that every named substance shares an effect.

| Direction | Examples on this wiki | What a sourced page is allowed to say |

| Neural / cognitive research names | Semax, Selank | No page yet. Do not treat the names as evidence. |

## Approved-product discussions versus experimental names

FDA's unapproved-GLP-1 page discusses semaglutide and tirzepatide as GLP-1 drugs that also circulate in unapproved versions, and it says those unapproved versions are not reviewed for safety, effectiveness, and quality before marketing. The same page says retatrutide and cagrilintide cannot be used in compounding, are not components of FDA-approved drugs, and have not been found safe and effective for any condition.

On the compounding-risk page, BPC-157, TB-500 (thymosin beta-4 fragment), CJC-1295, injectable GHK-Cu, MOTS-c, and melanotan II appear among nominations that were withdrawn. That is not an efficacy finding. Details stay on each substance page.

## Not peptides

NAD+, creatine, metformin, and rapamycin are often mentioned beside peptides in project notes. This wiki does not file them as peptides. A compound-record citation for each structure is not attached on this page, so this sentence is a filing rule, not a chemistry proof.

This page does not give doses, titration schedules, injection or implantation technique, preparation steps, or places to purchase a product.
