These sentences are taken from FDA's compounding-risk page and, where noted, the July 2026 advisory-committee agenda. They are not a finding that a substance works.

## What the opened sources say

- FDA's Category 2 table lists ipamorelin acetate under 503B, added 29 September 2023. The safety text cites immunogenicity from aggregation or peptide-related impurities, unnatural amino acids, and a published study of serious adverse events including death when ipamorelin was given intravenously for gastric motility.
- The same page's withdrawn table also lists ipamorelin acetate and says it remains in the Category 2 table because it is in Category 2 under the 503B interim policy. This page does not resolve that into a broader legal conclusion.
- FDA says it has not identified safety information for certain other injectable routes and therefore cannot tell whether the drug would cause harm by those routes.

## Not stated here

Legal effect beyond the words on those pages is not interpreted. No animal or human study is summarized, because none was opened.
