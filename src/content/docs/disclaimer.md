BioHack Wiki is an educational reference about research topics, devices, and regulation. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation, and it does not create a clinician–patient relationship.

Nothing here tells you to start, stop, or change a drug, device, supplement, or procedure. Approved product labeling and a qualified clinician are the right place for decisions about a specific person. Unapproved and compounded products are not the same thing as an approved product, even when the ingredient name matches.

The site does not provide dosing protocols, injection or implantation instructions, preparation steps, or purchasing guidance. Pages marked **Stub, needs sourcing** are unfinished and must not be quoted as findings.

Regulatory notes describe documents the editor opened. They are not a complete statement of the law in every country, and they go out of date. Check the agency's own page before you rely on a sentence.

If you think you are having a medical emergency, contact local emergency services.
