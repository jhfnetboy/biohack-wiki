BioHack Wiki collects descriptions of peptides, neural interfaces, prosthetics and assistive products, non-invasive neurotechnology, neuromodulation, aging biology, hormones, metabolic health, nootropics, sleep and recovery, wearables, and gene, cell, and do-it-yourself biology.

It is written for readers who want a map of the field and a trail back to primary documents. It is not a clinic, a pharmacy, or a product review site.

The English pages are the default. Simplified Chinese pages live under `/zh-cn/`. Where a Chinese page exists, Starlight emits `hreflang` alternates.

The seed favors narrow claims from U.S. FDA pages, a WHO fact sheet, an International Neuromodulation Society page, one prescribing-information file, and one PubMed record. Everything else is marked as a stub.

See the [editorial policy](/policies/editorial/) and the [medical disclaimer](/disclaimer/).
