This page exists so the lyophilized-peptide slot in the project list is not filled with a procedure. It records two FDA storage and contamination warnings that apply to compounded GLP-1 drugs, and it stops there.

## What the opened sources say

- FDA says injectable GLP-1 drugs require refrigeration as indicated in their package inserts. It says it has received complaints that some compounded GLP-1 drugs arrived warm or without enough ice packs, and it recommends not using an injectable GLP-1 drug that arrives warm or insufficiently refrigerated.
- FDA says compounded GLP-1 medication may be dispensed in vials that hold more than one dose. It says multi-dose vials of sterile medication are generally recommended to be used within 28 days after first use because contamination risk rises after that, and it recommends discarding the vial within 28 days even if drug remains and even if a compounder's instructions say otherwise.
- FDA also says to use a new sterile needle and syringe for each dose and not to reuse them, in that same compounded GLP-1 section. That is a contamination warning, not a technique guide, and this page adds no further steps.

## Not stated here

These two warnings are about compounded GLP-1 products as FDA described them. They are not a general rule this wiki invented for every lyophilized peptide. No solvent, volume, or storage temperature for any research peptide is stated.

This page does not give doses, titration schedules, injection or implantation technique, preparation steps, or places to purchase a product.
