The sentences below are limited to one FDA page opened for this draft.

## What the opened sources say

- FDA names tirzepatide among GLP-1 receptor agonist drugs for which people seek unapproved versions that are not reviewed for safety, effectiveness, and quality before marketing.
- As of 31 May 2026, FDA says it had received more than 730 adverse-event reports associated with compounded tirzepatide, with the same caveats it gives for compounded semaglutide: causality is not always clear, and underreporting is likely.
- FDA describes fraudulent compounded tirzepatide whose labels name pharmacies that did not compound the product. It says it is aware of one adverse-event report for such a product, with redness, swelling, pain, and a red lump at the injection site.
- FDA includes tirzepatide in the warning about unapproved drugs sold with research-use or not-for-human-consumption labels and dosing instructions.

## Not stated here

FDA discusses tirzepatide alongside semaglutide as a GLP-1 drug people seek in unapproved versions. This page does not state whether the approved products are single or dual agonists.
