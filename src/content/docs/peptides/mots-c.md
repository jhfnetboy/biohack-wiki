These sentences are taken from FDA's compounding-risk page and, where noted, the July 2026 advisory-committee agenda. They are not a finding that a substance works.

## What the opened sources say

- FDA spells the withdrawn-nomination entry “MOTs-C.” It says compounded drugs containing it may pose a significant immunogenicity risk for certain routes, that characterization may be complex, and that FDA has not identified human exposure data for any route.
- The 23 July 2026 agenda uses both “MOTs-C” and “MOTS-c” and says the committee would discuss the free base and the acetate for the 503A bulks list. Uses FDA says it evaluated are obesity and osteoporosis. No vote is reported here.

## Not stated here

Legal effect beyond the words on those pages is not interpreted. No animal or human study is summarized, because none was opened.
