The labeling file opened for this draft is the VYLEESI prescribing information revised in October 2026.

## What the opened sources say

- The highlights identify VYLEESI (bremelanotide injection), for subcutaneous use, as a melanocortin receptor agonist with initial U.S. approval in 2019.
- The labeled indication is acquired, generalized hypoactive sexual desire disorder in premenopausal women, with low desire that causes marked distress or interpersonal difficulty, and not due to a co-existing medical or psychiatric condition, a relationship problem, or a medication or other drug.
- Limitations of use on the highlights: not indicated for hypoactive sexual desire disorder in postmenopausal women or in men, and not indicated to enhance sexual performance.
- The highlights list uncontrolled hypertension or known cardiovascular disease as a contraindication, and a warning for a transient increase in blood pressure and decrease in heart rate after each dose that usually resolves within 12 hours.
