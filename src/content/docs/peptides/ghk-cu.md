These sentences are taken from FDA's compounding-risk page and, where noted, the July 2026 advisory-committee agenda. They are not a finding that a substance works.

## What the opened sources say

- The withdrawn list specifies “GHK-Cu (for injectable routes of administration).” FDA says compounded injectable drugs containing it may pose an immunogenicity risk from aggregation and peptide-related impurities, and that human data to inform safety are limited.
- This page does not review topical or cosmetic GHK-Cu. That use is outside the FDA sentence that was opened.

## Not stated here

Legal effect beyond the words on those pages is not interpreted. No animal or human study is summarized, because none was opened.

This page does not give doses, titration schedules, injection or implantation technique, preparation steps, or places to purchase a product.
