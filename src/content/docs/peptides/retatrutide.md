The sentences below are limited to one FDA page opened for this draft.

## What the opened sources say

- FDA says retatrutide cannot be used in compounding under federal law, is not a component of an FDA-approved drug, and has not been found safe and effective for any condition.
- FDA says it has warned telehealth companies marketing unapproved retatrutide, distributors selling it to compounders, and outsourcing facilities repackaging it.
- FDA also names retatrutide among unapproved drugs sold with false research-use or not-for-human-consumption labels, including products sold for human use with dosing instructions.
