The sentences below are limited to one FDA page opened for this draft.

## What the opened sources say

- FDA says cagrilintide cannot be used in compounding under federal law, is not a component of an FDA-approved drug, and has not been found safe and effective for any condition.
- The same FDA page's sentence about false research-use labels names semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide, survodutide, and mazdutide. It does not name cagrilintide in that sentence. This page does not add it.
