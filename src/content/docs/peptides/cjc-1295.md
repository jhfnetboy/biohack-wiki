These sentences are taken from FDA's compounding-risk page and, where noted, the July 2026 advisory-committee agenda. They are not a finding that a substance works.

## What the opened sources say

- CJC-1295 is on the withdrawn-nomination list. FDA says compounded drugs containing it may pose an immunogenicity risk for certain routes and may be difficult to characterize.
- FDA says it has identified serious adverse events associated with CJC-1295, including increased heart rate and a systemic vasodilatory reaction, and that available clinical data are limited.
