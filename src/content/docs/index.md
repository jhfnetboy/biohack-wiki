BioHack Wiki is a static reference. Pages either cite a source that was opened for this draft or they are marked **Stub, needs sourcing**.

A link from one wiki page to another is not evidence. Doses, injection and implantation technique, preparation steps, and purchasing guidance are out of scope.

The nine domains are an editorial map, not an official standard. Read [what this wiki means by biohacking](/overview/what-is-biohacking/) and the [three layers](/overview/three-layers/).

## How to read a page

The footer records the entity type, evidence grade, review date, sources, and pages that link here. Evidence grades stay **unrated** until a specific study design is cited. Regulatory sentences are limited to the document named in Sources.
