An open, open-source wiki helping any person or organization research and understand every area of biohacking and informing their decisions. Professionally tracked, continuously updated, objective and neutral.

It is not a clinic, a pharmacy, or a product review site. It does not give doses, injection or implantation steps, or purchasing guidance.

## How we work

- **Comprehensive coverage.** Nine domains, from biochemical interventions to lifestyle, with the same sourcing rules.
- **Decision support.** Information for decisions by any person or organization. Not medical advice.
- **Professional tracking and continuous updates.** Each page records a review date. Stubs stay marked until a source is checked.
- **Objective and neutral.** Evidence is graded from the cited study design. No sponsored rankings.
- **Open and open source.** Content is [CC BY-SA 4.0](/policies/license/). Code is MIT. Edit links go to the [public GitHub repository](https://github.com/jhfnetboy/biohack-wiki). The [contributing guide](/contributing/) explains how to add a page.
