This is a note about documents, not a complete legal opinion.

## What the opened sources say

- FDA says bulk substances nominated for the 503A or 503B bulks lists can be placed in Category 2 under interim policies when FDA identifies potential significant safety risks.
- The Category 2 table opened for this draft includes, among other substances, GHRP-2 (injectable and nasal, 503B, added 29 September 2023), GHRP-6 (503B, same date), and ipamorelin acetate (503B, same date). The table also lists substances that are not peptides. This page does not copy every row.
- A second table, “Bulk drug substances nominated but withdrawn,” says those substances were previously in Category 2 and were withdrawn by the nominators. Entries relevant to other pages on this wiki include BPC-157, CJC-1295, injectable GHK-Cu, melanotan II, MOTs-C, and thymosin beta-4 fragment (LKKTETQ), also called TB-500.
- Withdrawal by a nominator is not, on this page, rewritten as a statement that compounding is lawful or unlawful. Read the FDA page and the statute before relying on a legal conclusion.

## Not stated here

Dates of withdrawal are not stated because the opened page did not print a withdrawal date for those rows.
