WHO's assistive-technology fact sheet is dated 2 January 2024. The figures below are from that page.

## What the opened sources say

- WHO says assistive technology is an umbrella term for assistive products and their related systems and services.
- WHO says assistive products help maintain or improve functioning related to cognition, communication, hearing, mobility, self-care, and vision.
- WHO says more than 2.5 billion people need one or more assistive products, and that with population aging and noncommunicable diseases an estimated 3.5 billion people will need assistive technology by 2050. It says that in many countries most people who need it do not have access.
- Examples WHO gives include wheelchairs, glasses, prosthetic limbs, white canes, hearing aids, speech recognition, time-management software, and captioning. WHO says most users need more than one product.
- WHO publishes a Priority Assistive Products List. The items on that list were not copied for this draft.
