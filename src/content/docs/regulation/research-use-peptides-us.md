The project list asked for the U.S. legal status of research-use peptides. This draft only records one FDA statement. It is not that full analysis.

## What the opened sources say

- FDA says it has warned companies that illegally sold unapproved drugs containing semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide, survodutide, or mazdutide that were falsely labeled “for research purposes” or “not for human consumption.”
- FDA says those products have been sold directly to consumers for human use with dosing instructions, and that they are of unknown quality and may be harmful.
- A separate FDA guidance on research-use-only labeling, and the text of sections 503A and 503B, were not opened for this draft.

## Not stated here

Do not read this page as permission to sell or buy a product labeled for research.
