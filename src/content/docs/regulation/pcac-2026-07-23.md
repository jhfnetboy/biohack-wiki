Figures below are from the FDA advisory-committee calendar page for the 23–24 July 2026 meeting, as updated on that page through 14 July 2026.

## What the opened sources say

- The meeting is the Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee. FDA says advisory committees give non-binding recommendations. FDA generally follows them but is not required to.
- On 23 July 2026 the agenda is the 503A bulks list for BPC-157 (free base and acetate; use evaluated: ulcerative colitis), KPV (free base and acetate; wound healing and inflammatory conditions), TB-500 (free base and acetate; wound healing), and MOTs-C / MOTS-c (free base and acetate; obesity and osteoporosis).
- On 24 July 2026 the agenda is emideltide / DSIP (free base and acetate; opioid withdrawal, chronic insomnia, and narcolepsy), semax (free base and acetate; cerebral ischemia, migraine, and trigeminal neuralgia), and epitalon (free base and acetate; insomnia).
- The public docket printed on the page is FDA-2025-N-6895. The page says the docket closed on 22 July 2026.
- The calendar page lists briefing documents, questions, a final agenda, a roster, and FDA presentations. This wiki does not extract a vote tally or FDA's final listing decision from those files.
