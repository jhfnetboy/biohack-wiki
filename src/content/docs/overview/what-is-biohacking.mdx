On this wiki, biohacking names a cross-disciplinary filing system. It is not a claim that a regulator or a textbook draws one official boundary.

The ten domains are an editorial map. They are drawn in the [domain diagram](/diagrams/domain-map.svg) and listed on the [home page](/).

<DomainMap />

Project notes mention a humanities-journal split into do-it-yourself biology, health optimization, and technological enhancement. That paper was not opened, so this page does not say what it concludes.

Peptides are a first-level domain, alongside [biochemical](/biochemical/). Brain-computer interfaces are inside [neurotech](/neurohacking/). Prostheses are inside [bionics](/bionic/).
