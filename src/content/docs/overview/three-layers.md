![Three layers](/diagrams/three-layers.svg)

Observe is for measurement. Intervene is for an attempt to change a physiological state. Augment is for adding or replacing a function. A page may also set `layer` in its footer. These words are labels, not outcomes.

A later layer is not automatically better evidenced. See [evidence and safety](/overview/evidence-and-safety/).

Method, goal, and layer are three different axes. A page has one method domain, one or more goals (Restore, Enhance, Extend), and a layer badge. Extend is not a domain. See [goals](/goals/).

## Methods by layer

| Domain | Observe | Intervene | Augment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quantified self | Wearables, CGM, HRV | — | — |
| Environmental signals | Light and timing logs | Light, cold, heat, eating windows | — |
| Biochemical | Labs and assays | Small molecules and steroid hormones | — |
| Peptides | Identity and purity tests | Amino-acid signal molecules | — |
| Genetic | Sequencing and clocks | Gene therapy and editing | Durable nucleic-acid change |
| Cellular | — | Cells, reprogramming, tissue | — |
| Microbiome | Stool and metabolite tests | Microbes and transplant topics | — |
| Neurotech | EEG, fNIRS | TMS, tDCS, VNS | Implanted and intravascular BCI |
| Cyborg implants | — | — | A chip that does not read the brain |
| Bionics | EMG recording | Prosthesis training | The prosthesis or exoskeleton |
