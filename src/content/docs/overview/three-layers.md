![Three layers](/diagrams/three-layers.svg)

Observe is for measurement. Intervene is for an attempt to change a physiological state. Augment is for adding or replacing a function. A page may also set `layer` in its footer. These words are labels, not outcomes.

A later layer is not automatically better evidenced. See [evidence and safety](/overview/evidence-and-safety/).

## Nine by three

| Domain | Observe | Intervene | Augment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Biochemical | Labs and assays | Drugs, peptides, hormones | — |
| Neurohacking | EEG, fNIRS | TMS, tDCS, VNS | Implanted and intravascular BCI |
| Bionic | EMG recording | Prosthesis training | The prosthesis or exoskeleton |
| Cyborg | Reading an implanted sensor | — | The implant |
| Genetic | Sequencing | Cell and gene products | Durable genetic alteration |
| Longevity | Clocks and function tests | Research drugs and senolytics | — |
| Quantified self | Wearables, CGM, HRV | — | — |
| Microbiome | Stool and metabolite tests | Diet and transplant topics | — |
| Lifestyle | Sleep and light logs | Schedules, temperature, training | — |

A dash means this wiki does not use that cell as a primary file. It is not a finding.
