## Grades

`evidenceLevel` is about study design. A is a cited human randomized trial, B human observational, C animal, D in vitro, E anecdote, and unrated when that design is not cited. A frontier label is not an A.

## FDA pages opened for the peptide articles

FDA's unapproved-GLP-1 page says unapproved versions of drugs including semaglutide and tirzepatide are not reviewed for safety, effectiveness, and quality before marketing. It says retatrutide and cagrilintide cannot be used in compounding, are not components of FDA-approved drugs, and have not been found safe and effective for any condition. It says FDA has warned sellers of unapproved products containing semaglutide, tirzepatide, retatrutide, survodutide, or mazdutide that were falsely labeled for research or not for human consumption and sold for human use.

FDA's compounding-risk page uses Category 2 for nominated bulk substances it says may pose significant safety risks, and it lists other nominations as withdrawn by the nominators, including BPC-157 and the thymosin beta-4 fragment also called TB-500. Substance pages hold the narrower wording.

A meeting agenda or a withdrawn nomination is not a sentence that says "proven safe" or "proven effective." This page does not add a dose or a seller.
