![Closed loop](/diagrams/closed-loop.svg)

The picture is a safety frame, not a product that this wiki claims exists.

1. Sense: a wearable signal or a lab value.
2. Interpret: a person, or software a person can inspect, summarizes it.
3. Decide: a qualified person decides whether any change is appropriate. This wiki does not hand drug choice to an automated picker.
4. Evaluate: compare a later measurement and record uncertainty.

Notes about AI plus biosensing, neural interfaces, or biological engineering are not evidence that a combined system is safe. This page gives no dose and no schedule.
