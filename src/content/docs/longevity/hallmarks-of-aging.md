PubMed record 36599349 is the review titled “Hallmarks of aging: An expanding universe.” The twelve names below are copied from that abstract. They are a proposed framework, not a menu of treatments.

## What the opened sources say

- The abstract says a hallmark should show an age-associated manifestation, should accelerate aging when accentuated experimentally, and should offer a chance to slow, stop, or reverse aging when intervened on.
- The twelve hallmarks named in the abstract are: genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic alterations, loss of proteostasis, disabled macroautophagy, deregulated nutrient-sensing, mitochondrial dysfunction, cellular senescence, stem cell exhaustion, altered intercellular communication, chronic inflammation, and dysbiosis.
- The abstract says the hallmarks are interconnected and are also connected to proposed hallmarks of health: spatial compartmentalization, maintenance of homeostasis, and adequate responses to stress.

## Not stated here

No intervention on this list is endorsed. The NIA Interventions Testing Program page was not retrieved for this draft, so mouse-lifespan results are not stated.
