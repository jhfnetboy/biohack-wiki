Restore is the therapy and rehabilitation goal. These method domains are the ones this wiki tags for it:

- [Quantified self](/quantified-self/) reads the body before and after a change.
- [Biochemical](/biochemical/) and [peptides](/peptides/) add a chemical messenger.
- [Genetic](/genetic/) and [cellular](/cellular/) change instructions or living parts.
- [Microbiome](/microbiome/) changes the microbes that live with you.
- [Neurotech](/neurohacking/) reads or writes nerve signals.
- [Bionics](/bionic/) replaces or assists a limb or a sense.

This page does not recommend a treatment.
