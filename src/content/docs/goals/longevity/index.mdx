Longevity is not a method. It is the goal of postponing age-related loss of function, aimed at healthspan rather than years alone. The notes that used to live in a longevity domain stay here. Nothing on those pages was deleted.

## Notes kept on this goal

- [Twelve hallmarks of aging (2023)](/goals/longevity/hallmarks-of-aging/)
- [NIA Interventions Testing Program](/goals/longevity/nia-itp/) (stub)

## Methods this wiki tags for Extend

- [Quantified self](/quantified-self/) and epigenetic clocks under [genetic](/genetic/) are how age is measured, not how it is treated.
- [Environmental signals](/lifestyle/) include eating windows and other timed inputs.
- [Biochemical](/biochemical/) holds small molecules, including senolytics and compounds discussed around the ITP. [Peptides](/peptides/) holds amino-acid signals such as GLP-1 names and MOTS-c.
- [Cellular](/cellular/) is living cells and tissue: stem cells, reprogramming, tissue engineering.
- [Microbiome](/microbiome/) is where dysbiosis is filed.

This page does not claim a proven way to extend human life.
